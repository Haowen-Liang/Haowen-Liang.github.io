Architecture Summary

1. Two NIM Projects (2022–2028)

Governed under the countersigned Project Document "Supporting Effectiveness and Efficiency in the UXO Sector to contribute to the achievement of SDG 18 and Safe Path Forward III" ($25M total required resources):

  • UXO Lao Project (00120364 / Output 00116548): Implemented by UXO Lao under Outputs 1 & 2 across Xiengkhouang, Khammouane, Champasak, and Xekong (funded by New Zealand & Luxembourg).
  • NRA Project (00120365 / Output 00116549): Implemented by NRA across Outputs 1, 2, 3 & 4, including Army Unit 58 / Center 523 operations in Bolikhamxay & Vientiane (funded by KOICA, Ireland, Canada, and New Zealand).
2. Standalone DIM PIP Project (2026)

3RC-CCM Presidency Support (Project 01006631): Directly implemented by UNDP (DIM) with NRA/MoFA as Responsible Parties, solely funded by Australia ($106,196) to execute 7 priority preparatory, universalization, website, and youth activities for the 3rd Review Conference of the CCM in Vientiane (14–18 Sept 2026).
